# Quantified scoring model (use this at end of finals to engineer a target rank)

Derived from this 18:30 snapshot via `analyze_leaderboard.py` + `quantify_tiers.py`
(re-run on the clean 22:00 capture — just overwrite `round3_leaderboard.csv`).

## Fitted model — recovers the published formula
```
score ≈ 19.99 + 0.690*ReturnPct + 0.085*SharpePct      R² = 0.825
                 ^ formula weight 0.70    ^ weight 0.10
```
- **Intercept ≈ 20 = a clean book.** 0.15*DD + 0.05*Risk at full marks = 20 free
  points before any trading. (That's why flat +$50 P&L ranks 44th.)
- Working equation: **Score ≈ 20 + 0.70·ReturnPct + 0.10·SharpePct − (DD/Risk given back)**

## Three rules for end-game engineering
1. **Sharpe saturates at ~0.20** (only 2 players above it = ~98th pct). Past 0.20
   the 10% term adds nothing — target ≈0.20, don't chase higher.
2. **A clean book + Sharpe ~0.20 ≈ worth ~$30k of raw P&L.** Top 10 bar drops
   $74k→$41k; Top 25 drops $45k→$35k. Smoothness substitutes for ~3% return.
3. **At the very top, drawdown (the unseen 15%) is the separator** — player_001 beats
   player_002's +$703k with +$85k by having both max Sharpe AND near-zero DD.

## Tier sizing cheat-sheet
- **Finals spot:** non-negative P&L = done.
- **Top 25:** ~+3.5–4.5% ($35–45k) clean, Sharpe 0.10–0.20 — most efficient prize.
- **Top 10:** ~+4–7% ($41–74k) smooth, or 6-fig / +10%+ if choppy.
- **Top 5/3:** ~+4.5% at Shp 0.20 + ~0 DD ($44–46k), or brute +20%+.

**Caveat:** one 18:30 snapshot, percentiles are relative, field shifts → treat as
±1 tier, not exact. Re-run scripts on the final capture to refresh.
